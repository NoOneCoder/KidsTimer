Privacy Policy for Child's Time

Last updated: 15 September 2026

KidsTimer is a parental screen-time tool. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

Data we collect

KidsTimer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-up is required to use the app.
  • The parent PIN you set is stored only on your device, encrypted using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256). It is never transmitted anywhere, never sent to us or any third party, and never leaves your device.
  • No usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking SDKs are included in the app.
  • No network requests are made by the app at all. It functions entirely offline.
  • No information about your child — usage patterns, app activity, screen content, or anything else — is collected, viewed, or stored by the app or by us.

Permissions the app requests, and why

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): used solely to show the "Time's up" lock screen on top of whatever app is open when the timer ends. This is the app's core function — without it, the lock screen could not appear over other running apps.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): used to trigger the timer accurately at the selected time, even if the app is in the background.
  • Draw over other apps and pin the screen: used to prevent the phone from being unlocked or switched away from until the parent PIN is entered.

None of these permissions are used to access, read, or transmit any personal data. They control only the app's own on-screen behavior.

Data storage

All data (your PIN and the current timer state) is stored locally on your device only, using Android's standard encrypted storage APIs. Uninstalling the app permanently removes this data.

Children's privacy

This app is a tool for parents to manage a child's device usage. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children, since it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy
